Most of my recent work has revolved around RF Engineering, and Superconducting Qubits. My ability to build things has been recognized by numerous fellowships, organizations, awards, and press publications. In terms of formal training, I hold a S.B. in Electrical Engineering & a Political Science minor. 

I work best on open, candid, kind, fast-paced and collaborative teams agnostic of discipline. While I consider myself an engineer first... most things are interesting ~ I know a bit about econometrics, international relations, bioprinting, encryption, public policy, nanophotonics... :) 

I grew up in the beautiful Pacific Northwest state of Oregon with the loveliest rescue tabby cat "Amp". While I absolutely adore traveling + living around the world — 40 states and 21 countries visited so far — there will never be a place quite like home. 

I care a lot about supporting open-source projects, my local libraries and community colleges whenever possible ~ I encourage you to too!

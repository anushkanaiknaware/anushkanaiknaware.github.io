I consider myself a generalist. I find most things interesting ~ my previous work spans the gamut from econometrics to bioprinting to encryption to public policy to nanophotonics. I work best on open, candid, kind, fast-paced and collaborative teams agnostic of discpline. 

My ability to build things has been recognized by numerous fellowships, organizations, awards, and press publications. In terms of formal training, I hold a degree in Electrical Engineering & a Political Science minor (outside of projects/labs/discussions/teaching I did poorly at + hated my time in school). 

I grew up in the beautiful Pacific Northwest state of Oregon with the loveliest rescue tabby cat "Amp". While I absolutely adore traveling + living around the world — 40 states and 19 countries visited so far — there will never be a place quite like home. 

I care a lot about trying to support open-source projects, my local libraries and community colleges whenever possible ~ I encourage you to too!

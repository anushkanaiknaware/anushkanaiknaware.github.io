Most of my recent work has revolved around RF Engineering, and Superconducting Qubits. 

I consider myself an engineer first, but a generalist at heart... most things are interesting ~ my other projects span the gamut from econometrics to international relations to bioprinting to encryption to public policy to nanophotonics. I work best on open, candid, kind, fast-paced and collaborative teams agnostic of discipline. 

My ability to build things has been recognized by numerous fellowships, organizations, awards, and press publications. In terms of formal training, I hold a degree in Electrical Engineering & a Political Science minor. 

I grew up in the beautiful Pacific Northwest state of Oregon with the loveliest rescue tabby cat "Amp". While I absolutely adore traveling + living around the world — 40 states and 21 countries visited so far — there will never be a place quite like home. 

I care a lot about supporting open-source projects, my local libraries and community colleges whenever possible ~ I encourage you to too!

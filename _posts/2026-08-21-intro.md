My ability to build things has been recognized by numerous fellowships, organizations, awards, and press publications. While I absolutely adore traveling around the world — 43 states/territories and 22 countries — there will never be a place quite like the west coast. I care a lot about teaching + supporting open-source projects, my local libraries and community colleges whenever possible ~ I encourage you to too!

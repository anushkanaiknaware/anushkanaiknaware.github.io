Most recently I was an quantum hardware intern at a major US tech company + a researcher/ex-fellow with an international studies center. 

I consider myself a generalist. I find most things interesting ~ I have worked on several up and coming technology projects spanning the gamut from econometrics to bioprinting to encryption to public policy to nanophotonics.

My ability to build things has been recognized by numerous fellowships, organizations, awards, and press publications. I hold a degree in Electrical Engineering along with a minor in Political Science. 

I grew up in the beautiful Pacific Northwest state of Oregon with the loveliest rescue tabby cat "Amp". While I absolutely adore traveling + living around the world — 40 states and 19 countries visited so far — there will never be a place quite like home. 

I care a lot about trying to support open-source projects, my local libraries and community colleges whenever possible, and encourage you to too!
